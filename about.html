About Trade Africa Limited

Empowering African businesses to conquer global markets through expert consultancy and strategic partnerships

Our Story

Who We Are

Our Purpose

Trade Africa Limited aims to empower African MSMEs by simplifying international trade and fostering sustainable growth. We offer adapted consultancy in global logistics, market entry strategies, and executive development to help businesses confidently navigate new markets.

What We Do

At Trade Africa Limited we empower African MSMEs to expand globally by providing expert consultancy in international trade, logistics, and market entry. Our tailored solutions help businesses overcome barriers, optimize operations, and unlock growth opportunities for long-term success.

At Trade Africa Limited, our Code of Conduct outlines the principles guiding our behavior and decision-making. We maintain a culture of trust and accountability through regular reviews and updates aligned with industry best practices and evolving business landscapes.
