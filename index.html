About EGC Consulting

At EGC we empower African MSMEs to expand globally by providing expert consultancy in international trade, logistics, and market entry. Our tailored solutions help businesses overcome barriers, optimize operations, and unlock growth opportunities for long-term success.

Our Services

Personalised Import & Export platform

We partner with National Customs and Export Councils to create industry-focused trade platforms for SMEs to exchange goods and services.

Executive Development

We offer executive training and development workshops, symposiums and seminars to enhance SME's internationalisation and international trade knowledge and participation.

International Logistics Network

Eds Global Consult provides global logistics services that enable our clients to deliver or move their products across national borders.

Centralise Trade Procedure

Eds Global Consult handles the trade documentation process and fulfil all national and trade partner's legal requirements using its single trade platform while the SME focuses on production and service quality delivery.

Single Trade Window

Through our vast network, we create a funnel information platform that allows the identification and engagement of producers and buyers within industries leveraging EPZ and FTA platforms.

Business Development

We offer robust business development options tailored to our client's needs. Our development options include Business redesign and engineering, Corporate strategy development and deployment, Focused internationalisation strategy, Marketing and branding, and SME financing and financial management.
